Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Does any real food get delivered?

No. NeverEat is a satirical wellness simulator designed to scratch the late-night ordering itch and save your budget. However, we provide real step-by-step recipes if you want to cook!

Will my credit card ever be charged?

Never. The app uses a dummy test card (4242...). We do not ask for, process, or store any real payment details.

Do I need to register or create an account?

No account or sign-up is required. You can jump straight in and start ordering in under 3 seconds.

Is my data private?

Yes, 100%. We collect zero personal data, use no tracking cookies, and store your preferences strictly on your device.
